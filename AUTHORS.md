## Upstream authors and history

Vortex is based on the public [VIctory](https://github.com/Marvisak/VIctory) and [victory-gta6](https://github.com/fxceless/victory-gta6) projects. Their code authors, copyrights and original Git ancestry are retained. See [preserved upstream credits](docs/UPSTREAM_CREDITS.md), [license](LICENSE) and [development status](docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md).

GitHub automatically calculates its Contributors panel from the repository's commit history. The complete upstream ancestry was intentionally integrated into Vortex. An upstream author's presence in that panel is therefore not evidence that the author is a direct Vortex collaborator, member, maintainer, or endorser. Historical authorship must not be replaced with the maintainer's name.
